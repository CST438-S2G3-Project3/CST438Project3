## PR deadline

The course deadline is the outer limit: PR open by **2:00 AM Thursday**, reviewed and merged by
**10:00 AM Thursday**. Our own deadline is earlier:

- **Our PRs are open by:** `2:00 AM Thursday`
- **Our PRs are to be reviewed and merged by:** `11:59 PM Wednesday`
- **Why that time works for us:** `This time works for us because it gives us just about an entire week to work on a PR`

## Reviews

- **A requested reviewer responds within:** `12 hours`
- **A PR that needs changes is updated within:** `5 hours`

## Communication

- **Team channel:** `Slack`
- **How someone says they are out** (so the deputy can merge): `Communicate to the team using the Slack group chat that you are unable to contribute for the week`
